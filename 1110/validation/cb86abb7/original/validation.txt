Scope
Five OpenGL primitive APIs accept keyword-only segments (default 32, minimum 3).
Sphere latitude/longitude and arrow base/cap share the supplied resolution.
Cached meshes can be switched by the same instance name, preserving IDs and attributes.
Points, lines, USD, visibility/deletion behavior and mesh generators are outside this change.
