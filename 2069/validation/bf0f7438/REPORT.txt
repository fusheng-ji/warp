Publication and privacy
-----------------------
This supplemental bundle is published for the existing PR linked above.
Publication-state statements in older asset reports describe those historical snapshots.
validation.json records exact commands, UTC timestamps, durations and exit codes.
Log files retain test results and warnings; workstation filesystem roots are replaced
with variables. No credentials, hostnames, GPU UUIDs/PCI IDs or private email addresses
are intentionally included. Native binary hashes identify the locally built artifacts;
the binaries themselves and original workstation logs are not published.
